\# My project brief



Part 1: The question

Which settlement in Nyamagana sit in low-lying areas near rivers within 60m from river banks?



Part 2: Why it matters

Identify settlement which located within the river reserve boundary zone. The local Government can use this data to quantify the residents  which  affected by flood when the rivers are flooded during heavy rains.

Also use this data to determine rate of resident increment toward the river boundary which is contrary to environmental laws.



Part 3: The Data needed

Tanzania ward level data

Tanzania District level data

Tanzania regions boundary data

Settlement extent data - Mbugani\_Mirongo ward, Nyamagana District, Mwanza region

Waterway (river \& Stream) data

Elevation data\_ mbugani\_mirongo ward

Part 5: What I would build?



An interactive Mbugani\_Mirongo map that shows the residents located along the river, those located within 60m will be coloured red. The map will be updated after 3 months.
