Week 1 deliverable. GeoDv Lab Africa, Cohort One. Author: Devotha Kafuku

# 1. Project Title

GIS-based mapping and Monitoring of Settlements within 60m of Rivers in Mbugani, Mirongo and Pamba Wards, Nyamagana District, Mwanza.

# 2. Background

Urban settlements in Mwanza continue to expand as the population demand for residential land increase.
Some settlements are located close to rivers, streams and natural drainage corridors. During periods of heavy rainfall, river discharge incrased and runoff may expose nearby settlements to flooding.
GIS provide opportunity to identify settlement located close to waterways, determine their elevation and monitor the settlement development changes over time.

This project will focus on Mbugani, Mirongo and Pamba wards in Nyamagana District.

# 3. Project Question

1. Which settlements in Mbugani, Mirongo and Pamba wards are located within 60 meters of rivers and streams and in low-lying terrain?

2. How is settelement development  within 60m river-buffer zone changing over time?

# 4. Why the Project Matters

The project can provide useful spatial information for local government authorities, urban planners, environmental officer, disaster-managment stakeholders and communities

The results can support:

- Identification of settlement areas potentially exposed to flooding
- Monitoring of settlement growth close to river corridors
- Flood preparedness
- Urban-development planning
- Environemental management 
Communication of flood exposure information through interactive maps.

# 5. Study Area

The study will initially focus on three wards in Nyamagana District, Mwanza Region:

- Mbugani
- Mirongo
- Pamba

These wards were selected because they contain urban settlement along mirongo river and some streams
